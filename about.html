Who I Am

I was born and raised in San Francisco and moved to New York to pursue an engineering degree. While studying, I became increasingly drawn to learning about my cultural background and decided to switch to an art degree. I am fascinated by spiritual statues, collectible figurines, and all kinds of beautiful things. Making art is a way for me to see the creatures from my imagination.
